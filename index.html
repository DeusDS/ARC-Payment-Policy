Payment Policy: A/P and P-Card

A quick guide to how ARC pays vendors, partners and jurisdictions: which payment method to use, who has to sign, and what paperwork has to be attached. Based on the policy dated 3/5/2026.

The policy in eight points

  • Payments only. This policy governs disbursements. Buying authority comes from the Procurement and Contracting Policy, which applies first.
  • Three ways to pay: P-Card, A/P without a PO, or A/P with a PO (check, wire or EFT).
  • Every payment needs approval and a staff attestation that goods or services were received.
  • $5,000 is the key line. Above it: Chief Officer approval, a PO (unless a listed exception applies), and no single P-Card purchase without competitive procurement or a State Contract.
  • $25,000 is the top line. Above it: CEO approval. No P-Card limit may ever exceed it.
  • No W-9 or Vendor Information Form, no payment. E-Verify is required for vendors over $2,500.
  • ARC is sales-tax exempt. Never pay sales tax on an invoice.
  • Segregation of duties: no one person can request, review, authorize and execute the same payment.

    Card life cycle

    • Obtain: sign the cardholder agreement; Finance authorizes the card.
    • Buy: named cardholder only; keep an itemized receipt for every transaction, including refunds.
    • Reconcile: enter each transaction (purpose, cost center, account code, requester) and upload receipts in statement order.
    • Approve: Budget Manager, then Department Head signs the reconciliation; Finance reviews.
    • Pay: Finance makes one consolidated monthly payment to the control account.
    • Lost or stolen: notify Finance immediately. Leaving ARC: surrender the card to Finance.

    Special documentation

    • Meeting food: vendor invoice, meeting agenda and sign-in sheet.
    • Missing itemized receipt: cardholder reimburses ARC by the statement due date.
    • Travel: not on the P-Card except as the Travel policy allows; conference registration is fine.
    • Contracts: never on the P-Card; use the PO process.
    • Fuel cards: fuel only, with an invoice or ticket for every charge.

    A prohibited purchase means immediate, permanent loss of the card, reimbursement to ARC and possible discipline. Other exceptions go to the CFO or Deputy CFO, whose response can extend to termination.

    Disbursement controls

    No single person controls every step of a payment.

    RequestARC staff initiate the payment and attest to receipt.
    ApproveA separate person approves: Department Head, Chief Officer or CEO by threshold.
    ProcessFinance reviews the voucher package and executes payment.

    Positive Pay

    • Finance sends the bank each check run’s check numbers, amounts and payees.
    • The bank flags any presented check that doesn’t match all three.

    Checks

    • Never payable to “bearer” or “cash,” including emergency checks.
    • Voids: note in the check log and mark the check VOID.
    • Stop payments: enter in the bank portal, then in the ERP with a reversing entry.
    • Held checks: requestor signs and dates a copy at pickup.

    Records

    • Keep invoices, POs, contracts and approvals in the financial system.
    • Maintain an audit trail of who approved and processed each payment.
    • Retain per ARC’s records retention policy; flag 1099-reportable vendors.
